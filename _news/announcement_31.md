In addition to [RAISE](https://rutgersaisafety.org/) (Rutgers AI Safety Exchange), I am now the faculty advisor of the Rutgers Artificial Intelligence in Medicine and Healthcare Association, a new undergraduate club founded by Anudi Perera that plans meetings, discussions, and hackathons on AI for health. It is great to see AI communities growing at Rutgers. Undergraduates who would like to join can contact Anudi at [atp132@scarletmail.rutgers.edu](mailto:atp132@scarletmail.rutgers.edu).
